Signal Types

Three ways to listen.
One room.

Every room runs on a waveform. The mode shapes how tracks enter, move, and compete in the queue. Switch mid-session. The signal adapts.

~

Campfire

Sine Wave

Round-robin fairness. Everyone takes turns adding to the signal chain. Your track, then theirs, then yours again.

  • Tracks interleave by contributor
  • Votes are cosmetic (no reordering)
  • Anyone can skip
#

Spotlight

Square Wave

Host curates the vibe. Suggestions go to a pending queue. The host approves or rejects. Sharp. Defined.

  • Non-host additions need approval
  • Only host can skip tracks
  • Curated shield badge on queue
/

Open Floor

Sawtooth Wave

Democratic free-for-all. Votes reorder the queue in real time. The crowd decides what plays next.

  • Upvotes push tracks higher
  • Queue re-sorts on every vote
  • Anyone can add, anyone can skip

Real-Time Sync

Socket-powered playback. Everyone hears the same track at the same time. Latency-compensated down to the millisecond.

⚖

Control Voltage

Earn CV by contributing. Spend it on power moves — Overdrive a track to the top, Phase Cancel a skip, or Phantom Power a boost.

♬

Crossfader Duels

Two tracks enter, one track stays. The room votes. A/B showdowns that settle the vibe democratically.

⊞

QR Join Codes

Share your room with a 6-character code or scannable QR. No accounts required for guests. Walk in, tune in.

♥

Reactions & Chat

Fire, vibe, or skip reactions on any track. Live chat keeps the conversation flowing alongside the music.

⚙

Multi-Platform Search

Search across iTunes, Spotify, SoundCloud, TIDAL, and Last.fm. Connect your services and your library follows you in.
