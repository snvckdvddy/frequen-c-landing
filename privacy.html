Frequen-C Privacy Policy

Effective date: [set on first beta invite] · Contact: calebruble@gmail.com

Frequen-C is a small, independently built app for listening to music together in person. This policy says what we collect, why, and what we never do. It is written to be read, not skimmed past.

What we collect

Account basics. Your email address, a username, and a password (stored only as a secure hash). If you sign in with Apple or Google, we store the identifier those services give us instead of a password.

Music service connections. When you connect a streaming service (SoundCloud, Apple Music, Tidal, Last.fm, Spotify), that service gives us a token after you approve access on their site. We store that token so the app can search and queue music on your behalf. We never see or store your streaming service password.

Session activity. Rooms you create or join, tracks you queue, votes and reactions, and which connected service a queued track came from. This is the core of how the app works: shared queues need to know who queued what.

Crash and error reports. If crash reporting is enabled in your build, we receive technical reports when the app crashes (device model, OS version, what the app was doing). These help us fix bugs. They are not used for anything else.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data. To anyone. Ever.
  • We do not run ads or share data with advertisers.
  • We do not use third-party analytics trackers in this beta.
  • We do not store your streaming service passwords.
  • We do not record, store, or redistribute audio. Playback streams directly from your connected service through its own player.

Where your data lives

Account and session data is stored in our database hosted on Railway (a US-based hosting provider). Login tokens on your phone are stored in your device's secure storage.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Deleting your account removes your account record, your stored service tokens, and your session history from our database. This happens when you use Delete Account in the app's Profile screen; there is no waiting period and no "deactivated but retained" state.

Your choices

  • Disconnect any music service at any time in Profile, under Patch Cables. This deletes its stored token.
  • Delete your account at any time from the Profile screen.
  • Email us to ask what data we hold about you, or to ask for deletion if you cannot access the app.

Beta status

Frequen-C is in a closed beta with a small group of invited testers. Things will change, occasionally break, and improve fast. If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change applies to you.

Age

You must be at least 13 years old to use Frequen-C, and old enough to hold accounts with the music services you connect under their own terms.
